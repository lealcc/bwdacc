# bwdacc — Made for mobile

English narration. Product demonstration focused on ease of use and an interface designed for touch.

## 0:00–0:09 · Made for mobile

ComfyUI, designed for your phone. Write a prompt, explore your results, and keep creating with an interface built for touch.

## 0:09–0:19 · Within reach, in one place

The prompt bar stays at the bottom, within easy reach. Choose a workflow, aspect ratio, and up to four results in one compact menu.

## 0:19–0:29 · Tap to edit, swipe to explore

Tap an image and describe your change. Swipe through versions. Your last viewed version becomes the gallery cover, and the original is still there.

## 0:29–0:39 · From still to motion

Want motion? Open Animate on that same image. Add a motion prompt, choose your settings, and MiniMax H3 turns it into video.

## 0:39–0:52 · Save once, keep creating

Stop rebuilding the same references. Fragments keep a character, object, style, or place ready to reuse, with text, images, and model specific LoRA settings together.

## 0:52–1:04 · Make an image reusable

Turn an image into composition boxes, depth, or line maps. Tap an object to select it with SAM, then save a reusable Fragment. Support varies by workflow.

## 1:04–1:14 · Try, keep, pick up again

In Prompter, your draft stays ready for the next try. Keep your favorites, group them into projects, and reuse the full recipe instead of starting over.

## 1:14–1:25 · Mobile interface, ComfyUI underneath

The touch interface is native Android. A Windows connector sends workflow graphs to the ComfyUI API, tracks the queue, and brings results back to the app.

## 1:25–1:37 · Connect the computer, create on the phone

Pair your phone with the desktop connector. Use your existing ComfyUI installation or a separate one, with local network access and optional Tailscale when you are away.

## 1:37–1:47 · Less setup, more creating

Less setup between ideas. More creating on your phone. From a prompt to an edit and a moving shot, bwdacc keeps your tools together.
